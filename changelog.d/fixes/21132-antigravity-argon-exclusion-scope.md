- **fix(antigravity):** A quota refresh on an account without a Gemini 4 Argon quota now excludes only Argon instead of every `gemini-4*` model, and removes a stored `gemini-4*` that sits directly before `*argon*` (the pair earlier releases wrote). Neon, Hydrogen and Carbon requests on those accounts are no longer rejected with `No active credentials for provider: antigravity`; a `gemini-4*` stored anywhere else is treated as the operator's and must be removed by hand ([#21132](https://github.com/cheaper-inference/OmniRoute/issues/21132), [#21137](https://github.com/cheaper-inference/OmniRoute/issues/21137)) — thanks @dangphan122
